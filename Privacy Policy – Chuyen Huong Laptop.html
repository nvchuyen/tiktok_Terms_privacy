Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chuyen Huong Laptop (“we”, “us”) handles information when we use our internal publishing tool, “n8n_app”, to post our own laptop product videos to TikTok. Chuyen Huong Laptop sells new and used laptops and publishes short videos that show the products we have for sale.

Who uses the app

The app is operated only by Chuyen Huong Laptop. It is not offered to the public, and it does not publish content on behalf of other people.

Information we handle

  • TikTok account information. When we connect our own TikTok account, we receive basic profile details (such as account name) and an access token that allows the app to publish videos we have approved.
  • Video content. Product videos, titles, captions and hashtags that we create ourselves and send to TikTok.

We do not collect personal information from TikTok viewers, followers or customers through this app.

How we use it

  • To upload and publish our own product videos to our own TikTok account.
  • To keep the connection working (for example, refreshing the access token).

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties. Data is exchanged only between our automation workflow and TikTok through TikTok’s official Content Posting API.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored in our private automation environment, which only authorized staff can access. We keep tokens only as long as the connection is needed.

Your choices and deletion

We can disconnect the app at any time from our TikTok account settings (Security and permissions → Apps), which revokes access immediately. To request deletion of any data related to this app, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
